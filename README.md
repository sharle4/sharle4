## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🔹 [ode](https://github.com/sharle4/ode)
A project at the crossroads between **Letterboxd and Netflix — but for poetry**.  
It is a platform (website, soon an application) to access poems from all over the world in both **original language and translation**, with features to:  
- Rate poems, add them to lists, comment, and discuss with other poetry enthusiasts  
- Get personalized poem/author recommendations based on your tastes  
- Explore poetry as a living, social experience  

*Tech stack: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, modern web tooling.*

---

### 🔹 [wikisource-poem-scraper](https://github.com/sharle4/wikisource-poem-scraper)
A **dedicated module powering ode**.  
It is a **production-ready asynchronous scraper** that efficiently extracts poems and their metadata from Wikisource, supporting structured outputs (stanzas, verses, authors, categories), retries, checkpointing, and database storage.  

*Tech stack: Python, asyncio, aiohttp, Pydantic, SQLite.*

---

### 🔹 [lyricognizer](https://github.com/sharle4/lyricognizer)
An **attribution tool for song lyrics**, designed to identify the author of a song given its lyrics.  
- Automated data collection via the **Genius API**  
- Tokenization, normalization, and lemmatization of **1,000+ songs from 10 artists**  
- Predictive modeling using **Normalized Compression Distance (NCD)** to quantify stylistic differences  

*Tech stack: Python, NLP, NLTK/Spacy, text preprocessing, compression-based similarity (NCD), data pipelines.*

## 🔧 Currently working on

I am currently focusing on the **data engineering and curation layer of ode**, which involves transforming raw scraped text into a clean, research-ready dataset.  
This includes:  
- **Entity resolution and normalization** of author names to handle variations and duplicates  
- **Title validation and filtering** to ensure only canonical works are preserved  
- **Corpus structuring**: grouping poems into their correct collections and linking different versions of the same poem  
- **Metadata harmonization** to enable consistent indexing, querying, and recommendation workflows  

This step is essential to ensure that downstream tasks (recommendation, analysis, and visualization) are built on **high-quality, reliable, and semantically coherent data**.
